Privacy & Data Requests

Ask to access, correct, export, or request deletion of personal information associated with your Livestock Manager account. We will review the request and verify account ownership before disclosing information or making changes.

Important: Sending a request does not immediately delete a farmer account or erase server, provider or backup records. Legal retention obligations may apply. Do not include your password, identity document, card number or banking credentials in this form.

Already have an account? Sign in to request deletion or download your server records.

You can also contact accounts@livestock-manager.co.za. We aim to acknowledge requests within three business days; complex requests may require additional time. For details, read our Privacy Policy.
