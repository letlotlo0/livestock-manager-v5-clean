What Is a Digital Pedigree?

Understand where your animals come from, which animals are related, and how breeding records build a reliable herd history.

🧬 Digital Pedigree
THE MEANING

An animal's family history, recorded digitally.

A digital pedigree is an electronic family record for an animal. It tracks the animal's parents (the sire or father and dam or mother), and can also show grandparents and offspring across generations.

In Livestock Manager, your animal records are used to build this family tree automatically when the parent information matches other animals you have registered.

What a pedigree can show

  • Identity: animal name, tag or registration number.
  • Parentage: sire and dam, plus known grandparents.
  • Offspring: recorded calves or other young animals.
  • Breeding history: useful information for future mating decisions.
  • Registration: herdbook details if the farmer has recorded them.

Why farmers use it

A recorded pedigree helps farmers check family relationships, reduce the risk of close-relative breeding, follow desirable traits, and keep more complete livestock records. A digital record is not automatically an official breed-registration certificate; official registration comes from the relevant breed society or registry.

How to build yours

  1. Open Animals and register your sire, dam and offspring.
  2. For each offspring, record its sire and dam using a matching animal name, ID or tag.
  3. Open Family Tree and choose an animal to see the recorded relationships.
