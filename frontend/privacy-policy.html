Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

1. Responsible party

The responsible party for this service is Letlotlo Lesege, an individual merchant operating Livestock Manager in South Africa. Privacy enquiries and requests: accounts@livestock-manager.co.za. Public contact address: E2520, Lemenong, Phokeng, Rustenburg, North West, 0335, South Africa. Telephone: 067 990 7367. Privacy requests are handled through the contact address above.

2. Personal information handled

Depending on the features used, we process names, email addresses, authentication and session information, farms and livestock records, images uploaded by users, support communications, payment and subscription status, and technical security or error information. We do not need farmers to supply full payment-card numbers directly to the Livestock Manager record system.

3. Purposes for processing

We use information to register and authenticate accounts, deliver the requested farming tools, synchronise selected records, send account emails and alerts, process support cases, manage subscriptions and lawful payment confirmations, secure the service, and satisfy applicable legal obligations. We do not claim a verified marketing-consent workflow for optional marketing emails.

4. Service providers and cross-border processing

Livestock Manager uses external providers to operate the platform, including Render for hosting and storage, Resend for transactional email, and PayFast where payments are available. These providers may process or store information outside South Africa. We seek to handle international processing under applicable POPIA safeguards. Contact us to request further information about a particular provider or transfer.

5. Protection and retention

We use password protection, authenticated sessions, encrypted HTTPS connections and account-scoped access checks; no online service can guarantee absolute security. We retain personal data while needed to provide the service, respond to requests or disputes, or meet lawful accounting, security and regulatory obligations. We assess retention by the purpose and applicable law rather than promising a fixed universal period. If information is no longer lawfully needed, it is deleted or de-identified as reasonably practicable, subject to technical and legal constraints, including backup cycles. Subscription downgrade does not itself erase the farm data. There is currently no automatic self-service account-deletion function. Requests for access, correction or deletion are reviewed individually and require identity verification; submitting a request does not mean all copies are immediately erased.

6. Cookies and local browser data

Livestock Manager uses necessary session cookies and browser storage to keep users signed in and support farm record synchronisation. The app may store cached farm records on the user’s device; clearing browser data can remove those local copies but does not itself delete server records. Browser settings can control local storage, although blocking necessary cookies may prevent login.

7. Privacy requests

Subject to POPIA and other applicable law, individuals can request information about processing, access and correction, ask for deletion where lawful, or object to certain processing. Contact accounts@livestock-manager.co.za. We will verify identity before disclosing personal records and will consider each request in accordance with applicable law. The current software does not provide self-service account deletion, and receipt of an email request does not mean that all copies have been erased. Individuals may also contact the South African Information Regulator. You may also use the privacy request form. We aim to acknowledge support enquiries within three business days; resolution may require more time.

8. Updates and contact

This notice will be updated when service practices change materially, with notice where legally required. The platform is designed for individuals authorised to manage farm records, not for children to use independently.

Signed-in account controls

The Account & Data Privacy page allows authenticated users to request review of their account deletion and download server-held farm records. Requests are not immediate erasure or subscription cancellation. Device-only and provider-held data may require separate handling.
