1. Plans and billing

Livestock Manager plans currently proposed are Free (R0), Pro (R99 per month), and Pro+ (R199 per month). Paid checkout and recurring billing must first be activated and tested; customers will see the applicable price, renewal frequency and plan benefits before purchase.

2. Cancelling a subscription

When recurring billing is enabled, customers may use the subscription cancellation control where available or contact accounts@livestock-manager.co.za. Once PayFast confirms cancellation and the system records it, future renewal attempts should stop. Under the current backend logic, paid features remain available through the recorded paid-through date, after which access reverts to applicable free features, subject to law. We aim to acknowledge support requests within three business days. If cancellation fails, the customer should contact support promptly; do not assume cancellation succeeded until confirmed.

3. Refund assessment

To request a refund, email accounts@livestock-manager.co.za with the charge date, amount, relevant transaction reference and reason. Do not send card numbers, passwords or banking credentials. We assess requests individually, including duplicate or incorrect billing, failure to provide a paid service and applicable statutory cancellation or cooling-off rights. A refund required by law will not be denied because of this policy.

4. Refund processing

Where a refund is approved, we will arrange it using the original payment channel where supported, or another lawful appropriate method. We will communicate the decision and any processing information available. Provider and bank processing times vary; statutory timelines take precedence.

5. Access and farm information after cancellation

Cancellation of paid billing does not by itself mean a customer account is immediately deleted. When the paid period ends, paid-only functions may become unavailable. Exact access to excess animals, multi-farm records, data exports, retention and deletion after downgrade has not yet been fully verified and must be checked before this policy is published.
