COMMON QUESTIONS

Quick answers

I registered but didn't receive a verification email. +

Check your Spam or Promotions folder, confirm the address you entered and use the resend-verification option on the sign-in flow. If no message arrives, submit an enquiry below with your email address—never include passwords or verification codes.

I can't sign in to Livestock Manager. +

Check your email and password and confirm that your email was verified. You can use the password-reset option on the sign-in page. If you're still locked out, contact support without signing in.

Are my animals saved when I switch devices? +

Livestock Manager supports cloud-based livestock records. Use the same verified account and farm on each device. If a change does not appear, check your internet connection and cloud sync status before entering it again.

How do subscriptions and farm limits work? +

Free, Pro and Pro+ have different animal and farm allowances. The plan and active entitlements shown in your account govern access. Your recorded data should remain available if you downgrade, while some new actions may be restricted.

Where do I find my reports and backups? +

Sign in and open Farm Reports or your account's available export options. Financial PDF reporting is a paid-plan feature; raw-data backup access is intended to remain available. Avoid using PDF reports as your only restorable backup.

How do I report an incorrect record or payment? +

Describe the problem, the relevant date and what you expected to happen. Don't include payment-card numbers, passwords or API keys. Signed-in customers can track support-ticket status below.
